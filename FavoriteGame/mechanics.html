DEADLOCK

Home Heroes Mechanics Patch Notes

The mechanics of Deadlock revolve around strategic team play and communication. Some things can allow you to completely turn the game in your favor or let you practically secure the win.

The Patron is the most important objective in the game. You must destroy the enemy Patron to secure victory. The Patron has 2 phases - the first phase, where it attacks enemies with a powerful laser and the 2nd phase that triggers when it hits 50% HP, where it becomes temporarily invulnerable, reduces the respawn timer of the Patrons teammates and begins releasing powerful shockwaves that curse enemies.

In Deadlock, every hero can shoot, punch and parry. You have infinite ammo for your weapon, though it still requires reloading. You can also punch your enemies - there's a normal punch and a heavy punch. A normal punch is quick and harder to react to, but it deals less damage. A heavy punch deals far more damage, but requires a wind up and is easier to react to. Punches can be parried, by timing your parry correctly you will block the damage of the punch and temporarily stun your enemy, making them take more damage. Parries have a very distinct sound and a visual blue aura. If a player parries, but doesn't get punched, their parry will go on cooldown which can allow you to get a few punches on them before the cooldown goes off.

The other main mechanics are: farm, souls, death and items. Farm is the process of obtaining souls, usually by killing enemies or creeps. There are lane creeps that spawn every 30 seconds in groups of 4 and there are normal creeps that are scattered around the map and take a few minutes to respawn. Souls are the in-game currency which levels you up and lets you buy items. If you kill a normal creep, you will receive unsecured souls which will drop when you die. After some time, your unsecured souls will become secure, but if you die before that happens your unsecured souls will drop and anyone will be able to collect them. Upon dying, you will be unable to do anything except spectate your teammates and buy items. Death can be temporarily prevented with certain items, such as "Cheat Death". Items fall into 3 categories: spirit (magic), gun and vitality (health). Each category has a certain bonus for buying a certain amount of it. For example, if you have 4.8k souls worth of vitality items, you will receive 39% more health.

The urn is a comeback mechanic. It spawns every 10 minutes and carries souls. The urn has 3 states, based on each teams economy: unfavored, neutral and favored. Favored urns will spawn on the side of map that belongs to the team that is behind in souls and said team will capture the urn quicker. In order to receive the souls from the urn, it must be carried to a drop off location located in the middle left/right of the map. A king of the hill mode begins, the team that gets to 100% will have all the souls from the urn split evenly between them. The urn cannot be captured if there are other players contesting it, meaning you have to defeat the other team if they're nearby. If the urn is contested for too long, it will explode and the souls will scatter, letting any team take them.

In the middle of the map, there's a big and powerful boss, that upon being defeated will drop the rejuvenator, a crystal that can be punched up to 3 times and will grant revives to your team. The first time it's punched, it will fully restore the health of the person who punched it. Rejuvenators can be taken by anyone, so even if the enemy kills the boss, you can steal the rejuvenator. When you die with the rejuvenator, it will consume one charge and shortly revive you in the place you died. Once you got revived with the rejuvenator, you cannot be revived again, meaning each teammate will be able to use 1 rejuvenator at most. It is best to take down the boss after killing a large portion of the enemy team, since it will ensure that they won't be able to contest it and the rejuvenator will highly increase your chances of winning the next team fight.

Movement allows you to efficiently traverse the map, reach objectives and avoid enemies. Movement includes sliding, dashing, mantling, double jumping and boosting. You can combine these to enhance your movement even further. For example, if you mantle on a ledge and hold slide, you will slide further than usual. Actions such as double jumping and jumping consume stamina. Each hero has a different amount of base stamina, ranging from 2 to 4. Stamina can be increased by buying certain items or through certain abilities. The gray bars near your cursor indicate your current stamina.
