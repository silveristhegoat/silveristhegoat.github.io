Deadlock is an up and coming 6v6 game developed by Valve Corporation, combining two of the best game genres - MOBA and FPS. It is set in an alternate urban fantasy New York somewhere in the 20th century. The heroes take part in an occult ritual, where they have to kill their teams opposing god (Patron) to summon their own Patron and win the game. The game is currently in early development, with it being invite only since 2024.
